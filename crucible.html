Evidence confidence: —

Evidence confidence measures the strength, independence, and recency of evidence behind model inputs. It does not represent the probability the simulation is correct.

Confidence levels
  • HIGH — Multiple strong, independent sources support core inputs.
  • MEDIUM — Good evidence exists; some inputs rely on inference.
  • LOW — Important drivers depend on analyst assumptions or sparse evidence.

Observed company facts are sourced. Normalized state scores, scenario sensitivities, transition weights, momentum, and energy include explicit analyst assumptions where historical calibration is unavailable.
