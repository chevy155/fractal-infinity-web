Evidence confidence: —

Confidence measures the strength, independence, and recency of evidence behind the inputs — not the probability the forecast is correct.

Confidence levels
  • HIGH — Multiple strong independent lineages support primary drivers.
  • MEDIUM — Credible evidence; some inputs rely on inference.
  • LOW — Important inputs depend on assumptions or sparse evidence.
