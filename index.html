What We Do

Map the systems beneath advanced AI.

Fractal Infinity tracks the systems beneath advanced AI — compute, memory, semiconductors, photonics, energy, robotics, manufacturing, quantum systems, and autonomous agents.

We connect those systems to understand:

  • what is changing
  • what depends on what
  • where constraints are forming

We do not just report on frontier technology.
