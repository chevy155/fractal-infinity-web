About

A future-intelligence lab

Fractal Infinity is not a media site with a methodology badge. It is a lab that detects weak signals, stress-tests claims before consensus forms, inspects how AI research is produced, and publishes forecasts you can grade later.

The stack is simple to remember: Signal → Test → Research → Forecast. Episodes and social carry the signal. Simulation and the Research Lab do the work. Reports and Don't Read Until 2037 (Luna Ledger) turn what survives into dated, falsifiable forecasts.

Built for builders, investors, and engineers who need an intelligence layer on what comes next — not entertainment about what AI “will” do.
